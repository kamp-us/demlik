A stall that a Cmd handler's failure caused now rejects `driveToDone` with that failure, the same object, in place of `DriveStalledError`, and `onError` does not also hear it. Before, a handler's failure that has no caller to reject at, a throw on a later hop or any throw of a `Cmd.define`d handler, went to `onError` and the drive found nothing left to run, so the cause was lost. While a drive waits, the run now holds such a failure. If the drive stalls it rejects with the first held failure and every other held failure goes to `onError`. If the drive resolves anyway, because a Sub delivers the terminal Msg or an abort lands the cancelled State, every held failure goes to `onError` and the drive resolves exactly as before. A failure that arrives after the drive ended goes to `onError`, which now includes a first-hop throw of the settling State's Cmd that no one heard before. A store's refused save is not a handler's failure: it still goes to `onError` and the drive still stalls. A run you dispatch to yourself, with `run` and `dispatch`, is unchanged, and no export or signature changes.
